# Privacy Policy — Overnight Screen Light

**Effective date:** September 27, 2026

Overnight Screen Light turns your screen into an adjustable light. It is designed to work entirely on your device and does not collect any information about you.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app:

- does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data;
- has no accounts or sign-in;
- contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking of any kind;
- makes no network requests;
- does not share any data with third parties.

## What is stored on your device

The app saves only its own settings (your selected color, brightness level, and custom color) locally on your device so they are remembered the next time you open it. These settings never leave your device. Deleting the app removes them.

## Device features the app uses

- **Screen brightness:** the app adjusts your screen's brightness while it is open.
- **Keep awake:** the app keeps the screen from turning off while it is open.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

## Children

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted in this file with a new effective date.
